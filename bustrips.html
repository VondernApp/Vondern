Vondern Field Trips

Surprise Day Trips for Women Who Need a Break

Sometimes the best adventures are the ones you don’t have to plan.

Vondern Field Trips are curated day trips that take women to charming towns, unique local businesses, scenic outdoor spots, and fun experiences—all within a bus ride from the Philadelphia area.

Come with friends or join on your own. Either way, you’ll discover new places, meet great people, and enjoy a day just for yourself.

What’s Included

  • Comfortable round-trip transportation
  • A surprise destination (with clues beforehand)
  • Unique towns, shops, and local experiences
  • Plenty of laughs and new friendships

Why I’m Doing This

We all need a break from the routine sometimes. With the chaos of life, it’s easy to forget to do something just for yourself. Consider this your official permission slip to take the day off.

These trips are a fun way to experience what Vondern is all about—discovering new places, supporting small towns and businesses, and getting excited about exploring close to home.

Pennsylvania and our neighboring states are full of amazing places, and I can’t wait to share some of my favorites. I’ve always loved bringing random people together for a little adventure.

— Kristine
