Frequently Asked Questions

Is Vondern Free?

+

Yes, the app is free to regular users to download and explore.

Where does Vondern work?

+

We're launching with places within about a 90-minute radius of Philadelphia. As the platform grows, new regions will be added.

Will I Be Able to Explore Other Areas?

+

Yes! While Vondern is initially focused on experiences within about 90 minutes of Philadelphia, we’ll also highlight destinations beyond the region to inspire future travels and new experiences.

How is Vondern Different From Google or Yelp?

+

Vondern isn't built around reviews or popularity. It's a visual platform focused on experiences — helping you discover places you may not have thought to search for.

Do I Need an Account to Explore?

+

No. You can explore Vondern without creating an account. Sign up for free to save favorites, follow creators, and get recommendations based on your interests.

Do I have to post to use Vondern?

+

No, posting is optional, but we would love for you to share so others can enjoy!

What Kinds of Posts Are on Vondern?

+

Vondern is all about experiences. Posts highlight places worth checking out—from restaurants and breweries to parks, museums, farms, wineries, outdoor activities, and local events.
